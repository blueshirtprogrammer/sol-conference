# Spoken Cofounder Pitch

We are not building another AI app.

We are building the room that every AI app, phone, person and working agent can enter.

Right now, the intelligence is already here. ChatGPT can speak. Gemini can see and design. Claude and Codex can build. Phones can call, text, navigate and run the applications people already use. But every one of those capabilities is trapped in a separate window or device.

We connect them.

Sol Room is a local-first audio, video and device fabric. To ChatGPT or Claude, it looks like an ordinary microphone and speaker. To a phone, it looks like an ordinary headset. Underneath, we control exactly who can hear, speak, see, act and share.

That means a normal phone caller can speak with Sol. Josh can hear and talk with both. Gemini can join as the visual designer. Claude can supervise privately. Codex can build the real application while the conversation continues. When the prototype is ready, it appears on the shared screen and the room reviews it together.

We do not need to send every second of the meeting through an expensive multi-agent API pipeline. Local mode uses the AI applications, subscriptions, phones and computers the customer already owns. We sell the room: the fabric, hardware, installation, workflows, privacy, support and orchestration.

A Raspberry Pi 5 can become the always-on chairman of the physical room—managing devices, floor control, wake words, privacy buttons and lightweight local AI.

The first proof is simple: one phone, one laptop, one existing AI voice, one human and one clean digital bridge.

Once that works, everything else—multiple agents, screens, avatars, working teams and enterprise conference rooms—is another channel on the same fabric.

Sol Room is OBS for intelligence, Android Auto for work, and the operating system for the AI-native meeting room.
