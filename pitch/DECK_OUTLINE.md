# Sol Room Investor Deck — Narrative

1. **The room every AI can enter** — turn any phone, laptop, office or conference centre into a live AI company.
2. **The accidental proof already works** — a phone on speaker beside AI voice creates a three-way interaction; the problem is echo, control and scale.
3. **The category** — Sol Link, Sol Fabric, Sol Room, Sol Work and Sol Presence.
4. **The architecture** — standard devices and applications outside; explicit nodes and routes underneath.
5. **The phone stays the phone** — native SIM, contacts, dialler and apps; Sol Link controls approved actions and a validated headset transport carries audio.
6. **Multiple AIs on the line** — independently muted, isolated, conferenced or private channels.
7. **The five buses** — audio, visual, room, agent and artifact.
8. **The meeting does not end with notes** — Gemini designs, Claude reviews, Codex builds and the live artifact appears before the room ends.
9. **Builder marketplace wedge** — client criteria become live package search, visual shortlist and a working channel-partner application.
10. **Horizontal platform** — executive review, product studio, supplier translation, family support, training and conference centres.
11. **Pi-powered appliance** — the edge chairman for physical privacy, discovery, floor and scene control.
12. **Economics** — use properly licensed applications/subscriptions and local models; sell the room rather than mandatory per-minute inference.
13. **Commercial tiers** — Personal, Pro Room and Enterprise fleet.
14. **Roadmap** — vision/lab, desktop bridge, phone control, phone audio, rooms, visual/work, Pi and enterprise.
15. **The ask** — build the first bridge, prove the room and install the category.
