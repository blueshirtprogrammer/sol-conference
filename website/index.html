03 · Phone bridge

The phone stays the phone.

06 · Five synchronized buses

Media gets them into the room. Work makes the room valuable.

The design avoids pretending that an audio connection automatically grants tools or context.

Audio

Calls, voices, media, monitor, talkback, floor and mix-minus.

Visual

Screens, slides, cameras, Figma, documents and phone displays.

Room

Identity, roles, scenes, privacy and floor ownership.

Agent

Structured prompts, tools, private critique and delegation.

Artifact

Code, designs, decks, reports, previews, decisions and tasks.

08 · Powered by the edge

The Raspberry Pi is the room's chairman.

It does not need to run every heavyweight model. It keeps the room alive: discovery, scene state, USB/Bluetooth supervision, physical mute, wake words, speaker activity, local privacy and lightweight models.
